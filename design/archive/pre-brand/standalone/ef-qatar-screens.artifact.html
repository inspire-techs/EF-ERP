What membership gives you

Six things, all of them available from the day the platform opens.

Member Digital ID

A credential you carry on your phone and a partner can check. It works without a signal, and stops working the day your membership lapses.

Events and programmes

Seminars, workshops, sports and arts — booked, paid and ticketed in one place, with your ticket scanned at the door.

The member directory

800 engineers, searchable by name, employer, skill and membership category — and you decide what of yours is visible.

The job board

Vacancies posted by members and by employers EF has authorised, each one approved before it appears.

Partner offers

Benefits EF has negotiated on your behalf, redeemed against your membership rather than a coupon code.

Your documents

Every invoice, receipt and credit note EF has issued you, numbered in sequence and downloadable at any time.

Partners across Qatar can check an EF membership in seconds

A member shows their Digital ID; the partner sees only what EF has configured for that partner. Offer detail is available to members once signed in.
