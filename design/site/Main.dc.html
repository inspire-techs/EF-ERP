MEMBERSHIP

What membership gives you

Who may join

Member Digital ID

A credential you carry on your phone and a partner can check. It works without a signal, and stops working the day your membership lapses.

Events and programmes

Seminars, workshops, sport and the arts — booked, paid and ticketed in one place, with your ticket scanned at the door.

The member directory

800 engineers, searchable by name, employer, skill and category — and you decide what of yours is visible.

The job board

Vacancies posted by members and by employers EF has authorised, each one approved before it appears.

Partner offers

Benefits EF has negotiated on your behalf, redeemed against your membership rather than a coupon code.

Your documents

Every invoice, receipt and credit note EF has issued you, numbered in sequence and downloadable at any time.

FOR PARTNERS

A partner can check an EF membership in seconds.

The member shows their Digital ID; you see only what EF has configured for your partner type, and nothing more. It stops verifying the day their membership lapses.
