Membership

EF admits qualified engineers of Kerala origin resident in Qatar. Admission is decided by the elected committee against published criteria.

Ordinary Member QAR 500 a year
Qualified engineers of Kerala origin resident in Qatar
Associate Member Dues — EF to confirm
Engineering professionals in related disciplines
Student Member Dues — EF to confirm
Engineering students enrolled in Qatar
Life Member One-off — EF to confirm
By resolution of the committee, for long-standing members

How to apply

1 Apply with your qualification, your employment and a proposer in good standing.
2 EF verifies your documents against the originals.
3 The committee decides within five business days, and the decision is recorded.
4 Pay your dues, and your Member Digital ID is issued the same day.
